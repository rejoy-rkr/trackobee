Know who is working where, from any website.

Trackobee links the people who sign in to your website or app with the computers they sit at. See live availability by facility, screen-on time, and a login and logoff history for every endpoint.

Create a workspace Sign in

Works with any sign-in

Your backend makes one API call on login and logout. No SSO changes, no plugin for your stack.

Every endpoint's history

PC sign-in and sign-out, lock and unlock, offline and online, and website sessions, kept for at least 3 days (you choose).

Availability by facility

Map IP ranges to your sites and see who is available, away, locked or offline right now.

Roles and workspaces

Admins, sub-admins and read-only users. Every organization's data is separate and protected by database-level security.

How it works

  1. Create a workspace and add your facilities (IP ranges).
  2. Install the small agent on the PCs, or push the installer with your device management tool.
  3. Send login and logout events from your website with a single request:
curl -X POST "https://<your-project>.supabase.co/functions/v1/tb-events" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer YOUR_API_KEY" -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"event":"login","user":"priya","ip":"203.0.113.5"}'
Collected: computer name, IP addresses, OS user, lock and idle state, and the website user you report. No screenshots, keystrokes or content. Tell your people what is collected and get consent where the law requires it.
